# PulseVow

> **Understand the news, not just read it.**

PulseVow is an AI-powered Indian news intelligence platform that transforms thousands of news articles into **events, context, consensus, and actionable intelligence**.

Instead of simply aggregating articles, PulseVow connects multiple sources covering the same event and helps readers understand:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- Who is affected?
- What are different sources saying?
- What happened before this?
- What could happen next?
- How much signal is there versus sensationalism?
- What questions are people asking about the story?

---

## Overview

Modern news consumption is fragmented.

The same event may be reported by dozens of publishers with different headlines, perspectives, levels of detail, and degrees of sensationalism.

PulseVow brings these reports together and creates an **event-centric intelligence layer**.
